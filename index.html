My research

My research investigates mental health and its causes, particularly among young people. More specifically, my theoretical background is based on the network theory of mental disorders, which postulates that behaviours, cognitions and emotions can become causally reinforcing, leading to stable states that constitute psychopathology.

Professional Bio

Dr. Pedro Henrique Ribeiro Santiago is a psychological researcher whose work combines psychometrics, causal discovery, network science, and population mental health. His research focuses on understanding how emotional, behavioural, and social processes develop over time, with a particular interest in young people and evidence that can inform prevention, assessment, and care.

Research interests

  • Causal discovery methods for longitudinal psychological data.
  • Bayesian networks and directed acyclic graphs for symptom-level mental health research.
  • Markov Blanket models for identifying parsimonious predictors and intervention targets.
  • N-of-1 designs for person-specific mental health networks.
  • Idiographic approaches that connect causal discovery with individualized prevention and care.

Personal Bio
